# 📊 Blinkit Sales & Performance Analysis Dashboard – Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Blinkit grocery retail data using Microsoft Excel to understand sales performance, customer satisfaction ratings, item fat content contribution, outlet establishment trends, and regional tier performance.

The dataset contains 8,523 grocery items sold across different outlet sizes, types, and geographic locations.

The raw data was analyzed and transformed into an interactive Excel dashboard to generate actionable retail business insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to:

- Analyze overall revenue and total sales volume
- Track outlet growth and sales trends across establishment years (2012–2022)
- Compare Low Fat vs Regular fat item sales contribution
- Evaluate item type revenue performance (Fruits, Snacks, Household, Dairy, etc.)
- Analyze outlet performance by size (Small, Medium, High) and location tiers (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3)
- Compare outlet types (Supermarket types vs Grocery Stores)
- Track customer ratings and average sales per item
- Create an interactive Excel dashboard

## 💡 Key Insights

- Total sales generated across all outlets reached ₹1.20M with 8,523 items sold.
- The overall customer satisfaction rating across outlets is 3.9 out of 5.0.
- Low Fat items contribute a significantly higher revenue share compared to Regular fat items.
- Tier 3 cities drive the highest proportion of total sales across all geographic segments.
- Medium-sized outlets consistently outperform Small and High-capacity outlets in total revenue.
- Fruits & Vegetables along with Snack Foods are the top two revenue-generating categories.
- Supermarket Type 1 outlets account for the largest share of sales volume and customer orders.
- KPI tracking provides an instant overview of revenue, items sold, rating performance, and average ticket size.
